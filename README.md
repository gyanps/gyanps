## Hi, I'm Gyan 👋

I build things on the web. My main project right now is **Bharat Truck Simulator**.

### 🚚 [Bharat Truck Simulator](https://bharattrucksimulator.com) — *कुर्सी से सफ़र*

[![Bharat Truck Simulator](https://bharattrucksimulator.com/og.png)](https://bharattrucksimulator.com)

A free 3D truck-driving game that runs in the browser — no install. Drive a truck down an Indian highway from a first-person cab: dhabas, toll plazas, petrol pumps, cows on the road, phatak trains, and a seth who keeps calling to ask *"kahan pahunche?"*

**Play it:** https://bharattrucksimulator.com

**Ways to steer**
- Keyboard or touch
- **Your phone as a steering wheel** — scan a QR code, tilt the phone (WebRTC, peer-to-peer)
- **Chair mode** — strap the phone to an office chair and turn the chair to steer
- **Lean to drive** — lean in to accelerate, back to brake, tracked by the webcam on-device

**Highlights**
- 🗺️ Real highways from OpenStreetMap — 8 routes, drive from the start town to a real destination
- 🌐 Online multiplayer — auto rooms, crashes between players, push-to-talk voice chat, a shared radio
- 👓 Passengers — friends scan a QR on the screen and ride along on their phone, look around or use Cardboard VR
- 💥 Crash damage that changes how the truck drives, roadside mechanics, fuel, dhaba meals
- 🗣️ The driver talks in Hindi, English and 10 regional languages, picked by your location
- 📱 Works on phones and desktops, from low-end to high-end graphics

**Built with:** JavaScript · Vue · three.js · WebRTC · Cloudflare Workers + Durable Objects · MediaPipe · Blender · OpenStreetMap

The source code is private. The game is free to play.
